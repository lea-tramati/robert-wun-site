Becoming

Spring / Summer 2026

Becoming charts the transformative process of self-realisation. Using extreme silhouettes and heavily layered fabrics — organza over taffeta over crepe, each layer only partially obscuring the last — the collection explores the threshold state between what we are and what we aspire to be.

Each look functions as both a cocoon and an emergence. Figures are simultaneously enclosed and released. The process of dressing is made visible: unfinished seams, exposed boning and partial linings turn the interior of the garment outward, inviting the wearer — and the viewer — to understand construction as metaphor.

The palette moves from chalk white through ash and stone to absolute black, mirroring a journey from an undifferentiated beginning toward a defined, if still-evolving, selfhood.
