Fear

Spring / Summer 2023

Fear confronts anxiety and vulnerability through dramatic, enveloping silhouettes and an exploration of darkness and light. The collection draws on psychological archetypes — the shadow, the void, the threshold — translating them into structural organza volumes, ink-washed silks, and skeletal constructions that challenge the wearer to embody their own sense of courage.

The collection's opening look — a full-length gown with a 12-metre organza train, collapsed and gathered at the waist into a structure resembling a crumpled wing — became one of the most widely reproduced images of the season. It was subsequently acquired by the V&A Costume Department.

Fear argues that the confrontation with what frightens us is itself a form of beauty. The most extreme silhouettes are also the most serene.
