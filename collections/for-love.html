For Love

Autumn / Winter 2024

For Love is a meditation on the complexities of romantic devotion, rendered in silk organza and structured taffeta. The collection treats love not as sentiment but as force — gravitational, transformative, occasionally destructive.

Extraordinary proportions define each look: exaggerated sleeves that recall the wings of a bird mid-flight; cascading trains that pool behind the wearer like a second shadow; tightly boned bodices that impose a kind of beautiful constraint. The effect is of a letter written in architecture rather than words — equally a declaration and a construction.

The collection premiered at London Fashion Week to critical acclaim, with several looks subsequently acquired by international museum collections.
