Home Coming

Spring / Summer 2024

Home Coming is a return to origins — a celebration of the cultural hybridity that defines Robert Wun's practice. The collection asks what home means when the distance between worlds grows unbridgeable, and what it costs to carry multiple identities at once.

Drawing simultaneously on Cantonese opera, British tailoring and Parisian couture construction, the collection celebrates the paradox of belonging to multiple worlds at once. Kimono silhouettes are recut in heavyweight wool suiting; hand-embroidered silk panels are set into rigidly tailored jackets; lengths of fabric associated with ceremony and ritual are deployed in day-wear contexts.

Home Coming insists that hybridity is not a problem to be resolved but a richness to be inhabited.
