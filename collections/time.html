Time

Autumn / Winter 2023

Time is a meditation on temporality and the human relationship with memory. The collection asks: what remains of the past, and in what form does it persist?

Garments are deliberately deconstructed and layered — seams exposed, linings reversed, hems left unfinished — to evoke the texture of passing years. A monochromatic palette of white, grey and black reinforces the sense of images fading from a photograph. Fabric treatments include bleaching, intentional fraying and the application of archival inks that simulate the staining of age.

Yet Time is not a lament. Its final looks — all in white — suggest that the passage of time, however corrosive, leaves behind a residue that can be beautiful: a palimpsest in which every previous version of the self is still faintly legible.
