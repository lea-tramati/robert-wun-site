Valour

Autumn / Winter 2025

Valour explores the concept of courage through the lens of couture. Drawing inspiration from traditional Chinese opera costumes and military regalia from both East and West, it combines elaborate hand-worked surface ornamentation with rigorous construction techniques to produce garments that are simultaneously armour and art.

The collection takes its name from an old English word for heroism — not the heroism of conquest, but of endurance. Each look is conceived as a kind of wearable monument: a garment that the wearer inhabits as they might a place of significance. The silhouettes are deliberately extreme — dramatic shoulders, trained hems, cinched waists — imposing a physical awareness that commands the space around the body.
